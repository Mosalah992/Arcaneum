Northward, haunted

northern coastline

And south, Dwemer

live and toil,

A simple place,

a shield from draft.

At this stand

wield mage’s craft

So fierce the sea will boil.

Under Greybeards’

morning shadow

At this northern

watch, long lost

Nirn’s hoary Throat

does scrape the sky.

Here unleash a chilling cry

And gild this stand with frost.
